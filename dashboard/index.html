The token launch radar

Every protocol DefiLlama tracks, ranked by what its token would be worth at launch. A protocol counts as pre-token when its record carries no token — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is the record's word, not proof: a protocol listed here may still have a token its record does not carry.

How the radar works

The valuation is a power law, not a flat multiple. Across the 128 protocols in this snapshot that already trade, market cap grows with the square root of TVL — not in proportion to it — so a single multiple overvalues the big and undervalues the small. The model fits log(market cap) = acategory + b · log(TVL), where b is the least-squares slope over the live-token protocols (about 0.52 on this snapshot) and each category's intercept is its mean residual shrunk toward the global mean with a prior weight of 8 protocols. The core value of a pre-token protocol is exp(a + b · log TVL).

Readiness tilts the ranking. The board ranks by core value × readiness, where readiness is momentum (the protocol's 7-day TVL change against its own category's median change, capped at ±30 points) × maturity (saturating with age, 0.85–1.00) × chain breadth (0.92–1.00). Readiness never enters the backtest: the backtest scores the valuation core alone.

Backtest. Leave-one-out on the 128 live-token protocols: each protocol's market cap is predicted from its TVL with that protocol left out of the fit, slope refit included. Median error , mean error , mean log error , of predictions within 2× of the real market cap. The same harness scores a flat category-median multiple at 91.3% median error, 2147.0% mean error, 1.801 mean log error and 25.0% within 2× — the power law wins every column.

What this is not. The board never predicts whether or when a token launches, and an implied value is the model's claim about a launch at today's figures, not a price target. Farm plans are generic steps derived from each protocol's category, chains, TVL and age; they are not financial advice, and no step ever asks you to pay to claim anything.
